How to Play

1

Pass the Phone: Hand the phone around. Each player holds to reveal their role in complete secret.

2

The Secret Word: Civilians get the same word. Impostors receive no word!

3

Teammates: If there are multiple Impostors, they will see each other's names on their secret reveal screen.

4

Bluff & Vote: Impostors must pretend they know the secret word. When discussion ends, vote out the Impostors!
